Source: https://www.nexusmods.com/bloodborne/mods/460

The images show this layout, which the launcher binds when Keyboard is chosen:
E interact, Left Shift dodge, R blood vial, Q item, F transform, mouse buttons for attacks,
Ctrl + left click strong attack, Space L3, middle click lock on, G gestures, H items, Esc menu.
